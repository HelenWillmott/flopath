Before you decide

  • Each uni's grades are its standard A-level offer. Medicine offers are for 2027 entry. Psychology, Law, Computer Science, Nursing, Economics and Business were checked on each uni's own course page in October 2026, and you can tap a uni to open its page. The others are recent offers, and unis change them most years, so always check the course page before you apply.
  • The lists show a spread of universities for each course, not every one. UCAS lists them all.
  • Lots of unis make lower "contextual" offers, and many courses have a foundation year if your grades come in lower.
  • GCSEs count too. Most courses want at least grade 4 or 5 in English and Maths.
  • BTECs, Cambridge Technicals and T Levels are accepted for lots of courses, especially where no particular subject is required. Courses that need a specific science or Maths usually want the A-level, and some highly selective unis rarely take vocational qualifications on their own. Grades for these are compared using UCAS points.
  • Search real courses on UCAS and check subject advice at Informed Choices.
